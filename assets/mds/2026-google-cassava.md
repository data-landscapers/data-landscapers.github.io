# Could you dive deeper into Googles relationship with Cassava Technologies. Is there an MoU? Is there a quid pro quo for its investment?

The Google–Cassava relationship is deep, multi-layered, and has evolved rapidly since late 2023. There is a confirmed MoU, and while the formal terms are not fully public, the pattern of reciprocal commitments amounts to a clear quid pro quo across infrastructure, cloud distribution, and AI market-making.

## The MoU

Yes, there is a formal Memorandum of Understanding. It was signed on **10 November 2023** between **Liquid C2** (a Cassava Technologies subsidiary) and **Google Cloud**. The MoU formalised a strategic partnership covering three areas:[^1][^2]

1. **Data analytics and AI** – helping enterprise and government customers break down data silos and use Google's AI/ML tools.[^2]
2. **Cybersecurity** – enabling Liquid C2 customers to use the same security stack Google uses internally, specifically Chronicle and Mandiant solutions.[^2]
3. **Collaboration and productivity** – rolling out Google Workspace (with embedded generative AI/Duet AI) across Africa through Liquid C2.[^2]

Google Cloud's VP of Strategic Pursuits, Umesh Vemuri, framed the MoU explicitly as building on Google's **\$1 billion Africa pledge**.[^2]

## What Google gets in return

Google has never publicly stated a formal "rationale" for its equity investment in Cassava — TechCrunch noted that Google "didn't provide comment on the rationale behind the investment". But the structure of the relationship reveals a clear pattern of reciprocal value:[^3][^4]

### Distribution infrastructure for Google products across 30+ countries

- Liquid C2 became the **first Google Cloud Interconnect provider on the African continent** (June 2024), giving Google Cloud a private, low-latency on-ramp via Cassava's 110,000+ km fibre network across 21+ countries.[^5][^6][^7]
- Liquid C2 then launched **Liquid G** (late 2025), an AI-powered Google Cloud distribution programme that recruits and supports African resellers to sell Google Cloud services, with billing in local currency via the Cloudmania platform. Google Cloud's Director of Channel Sales (UKI \& SSA), Simon Aldous, publicly endorsed it.[^8][^9]
- Cassava became the **distribution channel for Gemini across Africa** (November 2025): a six-month free trial of Google AI Plus, plus data-free Gemini app access through Cassava's mobile operator network — effectively using Cassava as a continent-wide customer acquisition pipeline for Google's AI subscription product.[^10][^11][^12][^13]


### Physical infrastructure for Google's cables and cloud

- Liquid Intelligent Technologies (another Cassava subsidiary) **built the terrestrial segment of Google's Umoja subsea cable** across seven African countries (Kenya → Uganda → Rwanda → DRC → Zambia → Zimbabwe → South Africa), giving Google a land route it could not have built alone.[^14][^15][^16]
- Liquid also **acquired a fibre pair on Google's Equiano subsea cable** (2022), interconnecting Equiano's landing stations to Liquid's east–west African network — creating a new global IP route between Asia, Africa, and the US that benefits both parties.[^17]
- Cassava's **Africa Data Centres** subsidiary operates facilities in Nairobi, Lagos, Johannesburg, Cape Town, with expansion into Kigali, Lomé and Accra — providing co-location capacity that supports Google's cloud region in Johannesburg and future expansion.[^18][^19]


### AI model distribution and sovereign cloud

- Cassava's **Sovereign Cloud** (launched March 2026 at MWC Barcelona) explicitly hosts both **Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude** models on its African infrastructure, positioning Cassava as a multi-model AI distributor that keeps Google embedded in African government digital transformation.[^20][^21]
- Cassava purchased **12,000 NVIDIA GPUs** distributed across five African sites (including one near Cape Town) to power these AI services — creating "AI factories" that run Gemini among other models.[^12]
- Liquid C2 is positioned as one of **Google Cloud's largest Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) in Africa**, reselling Chronicle, Mandiant, and Vertex AI (which hosts Anthropic's Claude).[^22][^1]


## The implicit quid pro quo

While no public document lays out an explicit exchange of obligations, the pattern is unmistakable:


| What Google provides | What Cassava provides in return |
| :-- | :-- |
| Minority equity stake (part of \$90M round, Dec 2024) [^23][^3] | Balance-sheet strength + alignment of interests |
| Google Cloud platform, AI models (Gemini), security tools [^2][^1] | Continent-wide distribution channel across 30+ countries [^6][^8] |
| Subsea cable capacity (Equiano, Umoja) [^14][^17] | Terrestrial cable construction + interconnection across 7+ African states [^14][^15] |
| Six-month free Gemini AI Plus trials [^10][^11] | Mobile operator network to onboard millions of African users to Google AI [^10][^12] |
| Brand association with "sovereign" digital infrastructure [^20] | Hosting of Gemini inside African sovereign cloud for government clients [^20][^21] |

The equity investment effectively locks in Cassava as Google's primary go-to-market partner for cloud, AI, and connectivity across Africa, while Cassava gains capital, technology access, and the credibility of a Google partnership. The relationship is symbiotic but asymmetric: Google contributes technology and capital; Cassava contributes physical infrastructure, regulatory relationships, and last-mile distribution that Google cannot easily replicate on its own.[^3][^8][^12][^2]

## What remains undisclosed

- The exact size of Google's equity stake in Cassava (the \$90M round was shared with DFC and Finnfund; Google's individual contribution is not broken out).[^4][^3]
- Whether the MoU contains exclusivity clauses or minimum revenue commitments — Cassava publicly says it is a "multi-vendor" provider also working with Anthropic directly, Microsoft Azure, and others.[^24][^1]
- Whether Google has board representation or governance rights in Cassava as a result of the equity stake.[^3]
- The specific commercial terms of the Gemini distribution deal (revenue-sharing, conversion targets post-trial, data usage rights).[^11][^10]
