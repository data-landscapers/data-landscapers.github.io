<section class="cv-section">
  <div class="cv-section__label">Professional summary</div>
  <p>Proven expertise in strengthening data use and measurement ecosystems, institutional strengthening, and fostering strategic partnerships across African countries. Adept at designing and delivering projects on data and digital development and governance, with a strong focus on data integrity, responsible data practices, and actionable insights for evidence-based decision-making in public health and socio-economic sectors. Passionate about investing in local ecosystems and nurturing young talent, with a deep understanding of in-country complexities and a commitment to sustainable, country-owned solutions. A promoter of digital public goods and of the power of digital technology to change Africa for the better.</p>
  <p>For 15 years my work has examined the intersection of data, technology and society in Africa, particularly how data and digital technologies are upending Africa's societies. I have worked in research, advocacy, engagement and partnership-building across global health, demography, education and social protection, and have supported national and regional efforts to advance the Data Revolution agenda in Africa through research protocols, partnership and engagement strategies, and relationships with key African and Africa-based institutions.</p>
</section>
