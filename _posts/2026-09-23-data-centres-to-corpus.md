We first published a list of African data centres in [April](https://data-landscapers.io/lab/2026/04/15/africa-data-centres/) this year and manually updated it in [June](https://data-landscapers.io/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/). This work has now been [migrated into the Corpus repository](https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/datasets/data-centres/).

The dataset currently lists 421 operational facilities with a further 89 planned or under construction. Corpus sweeps for new sources every day. The table will be automatically updated whenever relevant new evidence arrives.
