Professional summary

Proven expertise in strengthening data use and measurement ecosystems, institutional strengthening, and fostering strategic partnerships across African countries. Adept at designing and delivering projects on data and digital development and governance, with a strong focus on data integrity, responsible data practices, and actionable insights for evidence-based decision-making in public health and socio-economic sectors. Passionate about investing in local ecosystems and nurturing young talent, with a deep understanding of in-country complexities and a commitment to sustainable, country-owned solutions. Fifteen years examining the intersection of data, technology and society in Africa, across global health, demography, education and social protection.
